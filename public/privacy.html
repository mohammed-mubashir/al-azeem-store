Privacy Policy

At AL AZEEM TRADERS, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or place an order, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number and delivery address.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to create and manage your account, process orders, arrange delivery, provide customer support and send important information about your orders.

3. Order Information

We keep information about your orders, including the products ordered, quantities, order amount, payment method, order status and delivery address, so that we can properly process and manage your orders.

4. Payment Information

Online payments are processed through our payment service provider. We do not intentionally store your complete card information or UPI credentials on our website.

5. Account Security

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure. Please contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to process payments, deliver orders, operate our website or provide related services.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use browser storage or similar technologies to remember login sessions, shopping cart information and other preferences required for the website to function properly.

8. Your Information

If you want to update your personal information or have questions about the information associated with your account, you can contact us through the contact details provided by the store.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact AL AZEEM KIRANA AND GENERAL STORE.
