Store Rule Book

Welcome to AL AZEEM TRADERS. This Rule Book explains the main rules and practices that apply to orders, delivery, cancellations, returns, refunds, and product availability.

1. Ordering

Customers can place orders through our online store for available products. Orders are processed after they are received and confirmed by our system.

2. Delivery

We do not guarantee same-day delivery. Depending on product availability, order volume, delivery conditions, and other circumstances, some orders may be delivered on the same day while others may take longer.

Delivery is free within approximately 4–5 km of the store. Delivery availability and timing may vary depending on the customer's location and circumstances.

3. Order Cancellation

Customers may cancel an order before preparation of the order has started.

Once order preparation has started, cancellation may no longer be possible.

4. Returns

We accept returns when there is a genuine issue with a product, including an expired product, a wrong product being delivered, or a damaged product.

Customers should report such issues within two days of receiving the order.

5. Damaged, Wrong, or Problematic Products

If an eligible product issue is reported and verified, the customer may receive either a replacement or a refund, depending on the situation and the customer's preference.

6. Refunds

Eligible refunds will be sent to the customer's bank account using the UPI system.

Once a refund is approved, the amount is generally refunded within a few hours.

7. Out-of-Stock Products

If an ordered product becomes unavailable, we will contact the customer first.

With the customer's agreement, we may offer a similar replacement product. We will not silently replace an unavailable product without informing the customer.

8. Retail & Wholesale Pricing

AL AZEEM provides wholesale pricing on the products available in the store. Retail pricing is also displayed where applicable.

Wholesale pricing applies to our products except for loose beverages.

Beverages are currently sold individually and are not sold by the carton.

9. Customer Feedback

Customers are welcome to provide honest feedback and ratings about their experience with our store and orders.

Feedback should be respectful and related to the customer's genuine experience.

10. Customer Support

If you have an issue with an order, product, delivery, return, replacement, or refund, please contact AL AZEEM TRADERS as soon as possible.

Providing the relevant order details will help us resolve the issue more quickly.

11. Updates to the Rule Book

We may update these store rules from time to time to reflect changes in our services or store practices. Any updated version will be published on this page.
